A.2 Recover Homographies — System of Equations

General formulation

From \(\,\mathbf{p}' \sim H\mathbf{p}\,\) to a linear system \(A\mathbf{h}=\mathbf{b}\).
\[ \mathbf{p}' \sim H\,\mathbf{p},\quad \mathbf{p}=[x,y,1]^T,\ \mathbf{p}'=[x',y',1]^T \] 固定 \(h_{33}=1\) 后，两条方程为： \[ \begin{aligned} -x\,h_{11} - y\,h_{12} - h_{13} + x\,x'\,h_{31} + y\,x'\,h_{32} &= -x' \\ -x\,h_{21} - y\,h_{22} - h_{23} + x\,y'\,h_{31} + y\,y'\,h_{32} &= -y' \end{aligned} \] 记 \(\mathbf{h}=[h_{11},h_{12},h_{13},h_{21},h_{22},h_{23},h_{31},h_{32}]^T\)， 则 \(A\mathbf{h}=\mathbf{b}\)。
